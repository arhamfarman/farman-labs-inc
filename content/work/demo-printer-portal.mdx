This is an internal demo build, not a client project. It shows the kind of system Farman Labs builds for a print shop, and the page layout every real case study will follow. Real case studies, with measured before-and-after numbers and client permission, replace it as they come in.

## The friction

A typical commercial printer takes orders by email, phone and WhatsApp. Someone re-keys each one into a spreadsheet and the job system, print files arrive at the wrong size or resolution and are found late, and customers call to ask where their order is.

## The system

Customers sign in to a branded portal, choose a product, fill in a guided spec form and upload their PDF. The file is checked for page size, bleed, colour mode and resolution before the order is accepted. The job then moves through a timeline the shop floor updates, and each change sends the customer an email.
